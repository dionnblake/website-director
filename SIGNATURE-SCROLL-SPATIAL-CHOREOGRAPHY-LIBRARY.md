## 1. Core Design Doctrine

### The Governing Principle
> **"Familiar enough to use. Different enough to remember."**

Website Director rejects the automatic replication of interchangeable "AI slop" website templates (hero banner, 3-column feature cards, centered CTA, customer logo strip, FAQ accordion, generic footer). 

Instead, Website Director investigates:
1. The business model and commercial reality
2. The target audience's cognitive posture and task urgency
3. The brand's tangible physical artifacts, materials, or operational choreography
4. The emotional trajectory and visceral impression to be created
5. The information hierarchy and evidence density

From this synthesis, Website Director asks:
> **"What spatial interaction or choreography would make this digital experience belong uniquely and irreplaceably to this specific brand?"**

### Secondary Internal Doctrine: "NEVER DEFAULT"
Do not automatically select the most common layout, scroll pattern, or interaction mechanic simply because it is ubiquitous or effortless to generate.

However:
> **DIFFERENT != RANDOM**

Novelty without purpose is an amateur design failure. An unfamiliar interaction that slows task completion without delivering superior comprehension, emotional resonance, or brand memorability must be ruthlessly discarded.

## 3. Pattern Taxonomy & The 18 Canonical Signature Patterns

The pattern registry is maintained in [templates/signature-interaction-registry.json](file:///c:/Users/ALPHA/Desktop/VIBE%20CODING%20PROJECTS/WEBSITE-DIRECTOR/templates/signature-interaction-registry.json).

### Pattern 01: PINNED_HORIZONTAL_SCROLLYTELLING (`PAT-01`)
- **Family:** `PINNED`
- **Behavior:** Physical vertical scroll input translates a narrative container horizontally while the viewport remains pinned in place.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Lateral progression across evolutionary chapters, curated portfolios, or chronological timelines without breaking the global vertical document model.
- **Best For:** Product evolutions, architectural portfolios, manufacturing journeys.
- **Avoid When:** High-velocity skimming or dense reference documentation is required.
- **Mobile Strategy:** `REFLOWED` (converts into a natural vertical card flow).

### Pattern 02: VERTICAL_HORIZONTAL_VERTICAL_JOURNEY (`PAT-02`)
- **Family:** `DIRECTIONAL`
- **Behavior:** Standard vertical document flow transitions seamlessly into a pinned horizontal world, completes an expansive chapter, and exits cleanly back into vertical document flow.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Signals a clear departure from standard reading into an atelier, vault, or product world, reinforcing physical entry and exit.

### Pattern 03: PINNED_SPLIT_NARRATIVE (`PAT-03`)
- **Family:** `PINNED`
- **Behavior:** One half of the viewport remains anchored with a persistent focal object while the other half scrolls technical or narrative specifications.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Grounds complex modular evidence against a constant visual anchor.

### Pattern 04: SCROLL_DRIVEN_ASSEMBLY (`PAT-04`)
- **Family:** `ASSEMBLY`
- **Behavior:** Scroll progressively translates, rotates, and locks individual structural components into a completed physical or conceptual assembly.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Visually demonstrates precision craftsmanship, engineering integrity, and internal architecture.

### Pattern 05: SCROLL_CAMERA_JOURNEY (`PAT-05`)
- **Family:** `CAMERA`
- **Behavior:** Scroll drives a virtual camera along a calibrated 3D path (dolly, orbit, room traversal) through a spatial scene.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Imparts physical scale and architectural depth. Governed by V2.1 WebGL runtime standards.

### Pattern 06: STICKY_CHAPTER_STACK (`PAT-06`)
- **Family:** `CHAPTER`
- **Behavior:** Consecutive full-width cards scroll upward, stick at top of viewport, and stack or reveal subsequent cards beneath them.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Establishes tangible progression across strategic pillars or service tiers.

### Pattern 07: IMAGE_TO_WORLD_TRANSITION (`PAT-07`)
- **Family:** `TRANSFORMATION`
- **Behavior:** An inset card or media item expands on scroll until it fills 100% of the viewport, seamlessly revealing the destination environment.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Creates cinematic continuity between catalog overview and deep environmental immersion.

### Pattern 08: SCROLL_MORPH_SEQUENCE (`PAT-08`)
- **Family:** `TRANSFORMATION`
- **Behavior:** Scroll scrubs vector paths, variable font axes, or geometric wireframes from one state into another.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Symbolizes adaptation, metamorphosis, or refinement.

### Pattern 09: PROGRESSIVE_REVEAL_CORRIDOR (`PAT-09`)
- **Family:** `REVEAL`
- **Behavior:** Viewport acts as an architectural portal where foreground masks part sequentially with scroll.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Evokes mystery, luxury, and entering archival sanctuaries.

### Pattern 10: DEPTH_SCROLLYTELLING (`PAT-10`)
- **Family:** `DEPTH`
- **Behavior:** Scroll moves the viewer through multi-layered 2.5D or 3D planes using scale, blur, and opacity changes.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Explores micro/macro layers, deep sea, space, or complex ecosystems.

### Pattern 11: DIAGONAL_SPATIAL_JOURNEY (`PAT-11`)
- **Family:** `SPATIAL`
- **Behavior:** Scroll translates the canvas along an intentional diagonal or isometric vector across an expansive conceptual blueprint or territory.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Breaks rectilinear constraints to express continuous flow across complex systems.

### Pattern 12: PINNED_TIMELINE (`PAT-12`)
- **Family:** `TEMPORAL`
- **Behavior:** A calibrated progress or date axis remains pinned while historical milestones scrub past.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Proves heritage, clinical validation, or long-term technological evolution.

### Pattern 13: SCROLL_DRIVEN_MAGNIFICATION (`PAT-13`)
- **Family:** `MAGNIFICATION`
- **Behavior:** Scroll moves continuously from an entire object view into extreme macro detail (e.g. building -> facade -> weave).
- **Narrative Purpose:** Proves material perfection and micro-tolerance obsession.

### Pattern 14: FIXED_FRAME_MOVING_WORLD (`PAT-14`)
- **Family:** `FRAMING`
- **Behavior:** An iconic physical aperture, lens bezel, or device silhouette remains fixed while environments scroll behind it.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Frames the world through the brand's proprietary instrument.

### Pattern 15: SCROLL_SYNCHRONIZED_STATE_STORY (`PAT-15`)
- **Family:** `STATE_DRIVEN`
- **Behavior:** Scroll directly inputs numerical parameters into an interactive state machine (e.g., biometric strain -> recovery).
- **Narrative Purpose:** Makes complex operational telemetry tangible and intuitive.

### Pattern 16: LAYERED_FOREGROUND_PASS (`PAT-16`)
- **Family:** `LAYERED`
- **Behavior:** Content translates between silhouetted foreground environmental elements at differential speeds.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Generates natural atmospheric depth without WebGL overhead.

### Pattern 17: SPATIAL_PORTFOLIO_WALL (`PAT-17`)
- **Family:** `NAVIGATIONAL`
- **Behavior:** Artifacts exist across a browsable 2D plane, navigating via smooth momentum pan and scroll triggers.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Encourages nonlinear artistic curation and discovery.

### Pattern 18: NARRATIVE_ZOOM_THROUGH (`PAT-18`)
- **Family:** `DEPTH`
- **Behavior:** Scroll travels through negative space in an aperture to trigger a conceptual leap into the brand's core.
- **Narrative Purpose:** Creates a dramatic conceptual rupture or milestone revelation.

## 4. Selection Governance & Evaluation Systems

### 4.1 Mold-Breaking Opportunity Assessment
During discovery and research, evaluate:
- `MOLD_BREAKING_OPPORTUNITY`: `NONE` | `LOW` | `MEDIUM` | `HIGH`
- A score of `NONE` is completely valid. Website Director never manufactures spectacle where conventional layouts are optimal.

### 4.2 Signature Interaction Level
- `0_NONE`: Conventional layout and standard vertical scrolling.
- `1_SUBTLE`: One restrained unexpected spatial moment.
- `2_FEATURE`: A signature mechanic defines a core section.
- `3_DEFINING`: Spatial choreography forms the central identity of the experience.

### 4.3 Creative Ambition Alignment
- `STANDARD`: Defaults to Level 0 or 1.
- `PREMIUM`: Evaluates Level 1 or 2 when supported by brand narrative.
- `SHOWCASE`: Mandatory evaluation of mold-breaking opportunities; at least one of the three pre-lock visual prototypes explores a signature spatial mechanic.
- `EXPERIMENTAL`: Explores Level 2 or 3 with explicit usability guardrails.

### 4.4 The 1 Primary + 1 Supporting Policy
To avoid cognitive exhaustion and visual chaos, an experience is strictly limited to:
- **Maximum 1 Primary Signature Mechanic**
- **Maximum 1 Supporting Mechanic**
- All other sections must provide calm, predictable vertical pacing.

### 4.5 The Originality & Brand Specificity Test
Every candidate signature mechanic must answer:
> **"Could 20 other unrelated brands in different industries use this exact interaction unchanged?"**
- If **YES**: The mechanic is generic template slop and must be rejected.
- If **NO**: The mechanic is grounded in the subject's authentic operations, materials, or geometry.

### 4.6 The Story Test
Every signature mechanic must complete the sentence:
> **"This interaction communicates [progression | transformation | assembly | precision | scale | depth | heritage]."**

## 5. Interaction, Usability & Accessibility Guardrails

1. **Never Block Standard Input:** Native wheel, trackpad, touch drag, keyboard navigation (`Space`, `PageDown`, arrows), and scrollbars must never be disabled or hijacked unpredictably.
2. **No Scroll-Jacking Traps:** The user must maintain full velocity control, with instant forward and backward reversibility.
3. **Orientation Invariant:** The user must always understand:
   - Where they are (`ACTIVE_CHAPTER`)
   - What is occurring (`INTERACTION_STATE`)
   - How to continue or exit
4. **Mobile Reflow Standard:** Heavy desktop pinned horizontal layouts must intentionally reflow into accessible vertical card sequences on mobile (`375px`).
5. **Reduced-Motion Fallback:** When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active:
   - Lateral scrubbed translation is disabled.
   - All narrative content renders statically in full vertical reading order.
   - Zero content is hidden or trapped behind skipped animations.
6. **Graceful JS / GSAP Degradation:** If JavaScript is disabled or GSAP fails to initialize, the DOM layout must render cleanly as a standard, fully accessible vertical document.
