## 1. What Awwwards Showcase Intelligence Is and Is NOT

``
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE AWWWARDS BENCHMARKING INVARIANT                    │
│                                                                          │
│  Awwwards is an external benchmark of craft, interaction, and visual     │
│  standards. It is NEVER a source of templates, never permission to copy, │
│  and never a substitute for client-grounded research.                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
``

### Clarification Matrix:

| Awwwards Intelligence IS | Awwwards Intelligence IS NOT |
| :--- | :--- |
| A high-quality reference pool for world-class web craft | Website Director's sovereign design authority |
| A source of interaction, layout, and motion patterns | A template library to clone or recreate |
| A dimensional benchmark (Typography, Hero, Motion, Mobile) | Permission to copy code, assets, or visual identity |
| A craft floor inspiration for ambitious creative projects | Proof that a trendy aesthetic fits every client |
| A portfolio art director evaluation lens | An official Awwwards-certified judging score |

## 4. Subject-Grounded Research Relevance

Awwwards research must align strictly with the confirmed creative_intent{} and industry truth:

- **Luxury Hospitality / Retreat:** Research luxury booking journeys, unhurried editorial storytelling, architectural photography presentation, and serene atmospheric transitions.
- **Bespoke Architecture:** Research spatial grid systems, full-bleed photography choreographies, and structural typography.
- **High-Performance Automotive / Horology:** Research mechanical precision, high-contrast monochrome renders, technical telemetry grids, and tactile micro-physics.
- **Enterprise / Deep Tech:** Research high-density data matrices, interactive computation tools, and surgical typography.

*Prohibition:* Do not research unrelated categories just because they look visually extravagant.

- **Animation & Kinetics:** Compositor-only transforms, smooth easing (power4.out), reduced-motion compliance.

- **Responsive Craft:** Flawless reflow across 1440px desktop, 768px tablet, and 390px mobile viewports.

> Website Director NEVER claims to be an official Awwwards judge, nor does it issue fake official Awwwards scores. This lens is an internal craft standard.

## 6. Anti-Copying & Deconstruction Synthesis

Website Director enforces the **Modular Synthesis Principle**:

`
  Reference A (Pacing) ──┐
  Reference B (Layout) ──┼──► [ Intent & Subject Grounding ] ──► Original Bespoke Creation
  Reference C (Micro)  ──┘
``

- **Rule:** Never produce *"A clone of Reference X for Client Y"*.
- Extract abstract transferable principles and re-assemble them through the client's unique brand truth, physical artifacts, audience psychology, and Design Constitution.

## 7. The Trend Contamination Check

Award galleries frequently suffer from cyclical aesthetic homogenization. Website Director aggressively screens candidate designs for **Awwwards Trend Contamination**:

| Trend Cliché | Why It Fails | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **All-Black Agency Aesthetic** | Inappropriate for warm, institutional, or daylight-native brands. | Ground palette in the client's authentic material world. |
| **Forced Horizontal Scroll** | Destroys cognitive rhythm and mobile comprehension. | Use horizontal scroll only for timelines or visual galleries. |
| **Giant Neutral Grotesk + Tiny Mono** | Has become the universal generic template of award sites. | Select bespoke display typography with real personality. |
| **Gratuitous WebGL Blobs** | Unmotivated 3D blobs waste GPU cycles with zero brand meaning. | Ground 3D/canvas strictly in functional product storytelling. |
| **Cursor Follower Circles** | Disorients users on standard information websites. | Limit micro-physics to subtle magnetic pulls on primary CTAs. |
| **Preloader Screens on Static Sites** | Creates artificial friction and delays first contentful paint. | Render instant hero content without blocking splash screens. |

## 8. Graceful Fallback & Availability Resilience

If Awwwards is unreachable, automated browsing is blocked, or page structure changes:
1. Record site-profile.json → 
esearch.showcase_research_status = "PARTIAL" or "UNAVAILABLE".
2. Fall back gracefully to other curated repositories (Landbook, Typewolf, Siteinspire, direct brand URLs).
3. **Absolute Evidence Rule:** NEVER hallucinate Awwwards winners, scores, site names, or URLs. Record only verified, fetchable references.
