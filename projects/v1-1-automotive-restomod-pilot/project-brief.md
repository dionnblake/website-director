## 1. Business Identity & Nature
- **Business Name:** KREISLER & VOSS Coachbuilding & Motorenwerke
- **Industry / Category:** Bespoke Automotive Restomod, Coachbuilding & Mechanical Engineering Atelier.
- **Locations:** Cologne (Engineering & Powertrain Facility) • Black Forest (Chassis Dynamics & Road Testing) • Modena (Coachbuilding & Interior Joinery).
- **Core Specialization:** Hand-crafted, re-engineered analog grand tourers and air-cooled sports cars. Precision structural restoration, modernized naturally aspirated powertrains, custom aluminum coachwork, bespoke milled billet switchgear, and saddle-stitched Bridge of Weir leather interiors.
- **Annual Production:** Exactly 8 individually commissioned vehicles per year.
- **Commission Level:** $240,000 – $650,000+ (donor vehicle + bespoke coachbuilding + engineering).

## 2. Founders & Heritage (Fictional Business Fact)
- **Markus Kreisler:** Master Powertrain & Chassis Engineer (ex-Weissach Motorsport development). Focus: Balanced mechanical purity, dry-sump lubrication, harmonic induction tuning, analog feedback.
- **Dominic Voss:** Coachbuilder & Spatial Architect (ex-Milanese coachbuilding houses). Focus: Hand-wheeled aluminum body panels, precision billet jewelry, tactile cockpit ergonomics.

## 3. Primary Audience & Psychology
- **Target Customer:** Affluent automotive connoisseurs, designers, collectors, and engineers (ages 35–65).
- **Customer Desires:** Mechanical authenticity, tactile sensory satisfaction, analog driving joy, enduring craftsmanship, engineering transparency.
- **Customer Dislikes:** Aggressive supercar depreciation, electronic screen overload, fake carbon cosmetic wraps, dealership sales pressure, hype culture.

## 4. Primary Conversion Goal
- **Conversion Goal:** Request a Private Atelier Consultation / Commission Dialogue.
- **Tone:** Dignified, highly considered, private technical discussion between patron and master builder.
- **CTA:** `Initiate Commission Dialogue` / `Request Atelier Build Consultation`.
