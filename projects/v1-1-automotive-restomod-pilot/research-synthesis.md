## 1. Industry Reality
Affluent automotive patrons commissioning $240K–$650K+ bespoke restomods expect immediate evidence of mechanical mastery, obsessive craft, and engineering transparency. They are collectors and drivers who reject digital infotainment screens in favor of analog sensory satisfaction (tactile switchgear, naturally aspirated induction sound, mechanical gearshifts). They demand dignity, clear engineering data, and an unhurried, private commission dialogue.

---

## 2. Industry Clichés to Avoid
1. **The Fast & Furious Neon Trap:** Aggressive neon glow, fake tire smoke, and racing game UI tropes.
2. **The Generic Dealership Funnel:** "Check Inventory", "Get Financing", and aggressive pop-ups that degrade an artisanal atelier into a used car lot.
3. **The Uncontrollable 3D Canvas:** Overheating WebGL models with laggy camera physics that disorient visitors.
4. **The Generic Obsidian Monochrome:** Cold, lifeless black-on-black carbon fiber backgrounds with zero tactile or emotional warmth.

---

## 3. Best-in-Class Lessons
- **From Singer Vehicle Design:** Atmospheric lighting, quiet luxury restraint, and treating mechanical components (milled gauges, exposed linkage) as haute horlogerie jewelry.
- **From Alfaholics:** Radical engineering transparency (dyno power curves, uniball suspension kinematics, corner weights, gear ratios).
- **From A. Lange & Söhne:** Macro metallography, exploded mechanical schematics, and directional raking lighting on milled metal.
- **From Landbook & Minimal Gallery:** Scroll-driven progressive illumination heroes, tactile material laboratories, and dual-perspective (Photo ↔ Blueprint) case studies.

---

## 4. Design Opportunity & Whitespace
**"The Milled Mechanical Atelier"**  
While typical sports car sites are either noisy commercial dealerships or sterile dark galleries, KREISLER & VOSS will occupy the distinct whitespace of **Precision Mechanical Monograph + Interactive Engineering Laboratory**.

The digital presence will synthesize:
- A rich, weighted gunmetal canvas (`#0E0F12`) illuminated by natural tungsten workshop light and warm saddle leather accents (`#C88242`).
- High-impact architectural grotesque headlines (`Syne` / `Cabinet Grotesk`) paired with Swiss sans body text and precision monospace metadata (`JetBrains Mono`).
- A **scroll-driven cinematic assembly hero** (Motion Level 3) progressing from a macro milled intake trumpet through chassis form to the full grand tourer vehicle reveal.
- An interactive **"Mechanical & Material Atelier"** allowing visitors to inspect billet intake runners, Connolly saddle leather, titanium exhaust manifolds, and double-wishbone suspension geometry.
- Interactive **dual-perspective case studies** (switching between spatial workshop photography and technical CAD engineering blueprints / dyno curves).
- A dignified **multi-step commission consultation suite**.

## 7. Principles Rejected & Anti-Homogenization

### Against Alpha Starts Now (ASN):
- **Rejected:** Monospaced plate numbering `[ASN-01]`, engineering crosshairs, dark industrial enterprise software monograph, burnt oxide accent.
- **KREISLER & VOSS Difference:** Coachbuilt automotive art, warm saddle leather accents, high-contrast grotesque typography, automotive dyno curves and mechanical assembly.

### Against Valentin & Hesse (V&H):
- **Rejected:** Warm limestone alabaster canvas (`#F8F6F0`), Cormorant Garamond display serif, Swiss architectural blueprint drawer.
- **KREISLER & VOSS Difference:** Deep gunmetal workshop atmosphere (`#0E0F12`), precision geometric sans display (`Syne`), milled automotive components and dyno power schematics.
