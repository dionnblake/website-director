## 1. Archetype Formula
- **Primary (50%): Industrial Mechanical Modernist** — Uncompromising structural precision, raw CNC-milled surfaces, exposed mechanical linkage, and metallurgical honesty.
- **Secondary (30%): Editorial Monograph** — Curatorial layout cadence, generous asymmetric white space, high-contrast expressive display typography, and fine-art automotive prose.
- **Accent (20%): Precision Technical Drafting** — Dyno torque curves, suspension kinematics, gear ratio charts, and engineering blueprint overlays.

---

## 2. Visual & Sensory Concept: "The Milled Machine"
The interface evokes stepping into an immaculate private engineering atelier at dusk. Deep gunmetal slate surfaces (`#0E0F12`) absorb ambient darkness, while directional tungsten raking light catches the beveled edges of milled 6061-T6 aluminum components, hand-wheeled coachwork, and vegetable-tanned saddle leather cockpit appointments.

---

## 3. Density & Layout System
- **Density:** Balanced-Spacious (generous spatial margins: `clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 7.5rem)`).
- **Layout Grid:** 12-column asymmetric mechanical grid with dynamic offset pairings.
- **Geometry:** Precision milled sharp geometry (`radius: 1px` for technical controls, `0px` for structural panels).

---

## 4. Typography Expression
- **Display:** `Syne` / `Cabinet Grotesk` (Weight: 600/700, Tracking: `-0.025em`) for powerful, sculptural mechanical headlines.
- **Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weight: 400/500, Line-height: `1.65`) for effortless editorial clarity.
- **Technical Metadata:** `JetBrains Mono` (Weight: 500, Letter-spacing: `0.08em`) for precision telemetry and engineering specifications.

---

## 5. Color Moodboard & Material Ratios
- **Surface Canvas (60%):** `#0E0F12` (Milled Gunmetal) & `#17191E` (Alloy Slate).
- **Text & Pigments (30%):** `#F2F1ED` (Platinum White) & `#8B8E96` (Machinist Titanium).
- **Primary Accent (10%):** `#C88242` (Warm Saddle Ochre / Cognac Leather) with `#388E3C` (Dyno Green telemetry status).
