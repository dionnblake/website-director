## 2. Industry Reality & Clichés Synthesis

- **What Affluent Restomod Patrons Look For:**
  1. Instant proof of engineering integrity (drivetrain specs, dyno curves, chassis stiffness, suspension geometry).
  2. Sensual appreciation of physical craft (hand-formed aluminum, billet switchgear, Connolly/Bridge of Weir leather).
  3. Clear, unhurried commission pathway (how a build slot is reserved and personalized).
  4. Absolute visual composure (museum-grade art direction, not noisy sales hype).

- **Industry Clichés to Reject:**
  1. *The Fast & Furious Neon Trap:* Aggressive high-saturation neon lights, fake smoke, and gaming sound effects.
  2. *The Dealership Spec Sheet:* Cluttered tables of standard features and "Get a Quote" popups.
  3. *The Uncontrollable 3D Model:* Heavy WebGL canvases that overheat laptops and spin erratically.
  4. *The Dark Obsidian Monochrome:* Generic black-on-black carbon fiber textures with zero emotional warmth.
