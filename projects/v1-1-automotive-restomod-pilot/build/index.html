ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

In Defense of the Tactile Machine

In an era where modern performance cars have become digital computers muted by synthetic sound generators, artificial steering weight, and intrusive electronic intervention, KREISLER & VOSS preserves the visceral joy of analog speed.

Every commission is a masterwork of dry-sumped mechanical induction, direct rack-and-pinion steering feedback, and hand-stitched leather intimacy. We do not build digital illusions; we build permanent, tactile machines.

01

Naturally Aspirated Induction

Independent throttle bodies, custom-tuned velocity stacks, and 8,500 RPM harmonic air-cooled induction. Zero turbo lag, instant throttle modulation, and mechanical acoustic purity.

02

Hand-Formed Coachbuilding

Body panels shaped by master artisans over timber bucks in Modena using traditional English wheeling machines and 1.2mm aerospace aluminum alloy.

03

Analog Cockpit Ergonomics

CNC-machined aluminum switchgear with mechanical click detents, analog chronometer gauges, exposed gearshift linkages, and zero digital touchscreen distractions.

BESPOKE LIFECYCLE

The Four-Stage Commission

An unhurried collaboration between patron, engineer, and coachbuilder taking between 14 to 22 months from donor acquisition to the inaugural alpine shakedown.

PHASE 01

Donor Sourcing & 3D Scanning

We source an authentic, matching-numbers donor monocoque or accept a patron's heirloom vehicle. The entire chassis is laser-scanned to 0.02mm accuracy to map geometric baseline alignment.

PHASE 02

Powertrain & Dynamic Blueprinting

The engine is blueprinted by hand in Cologne with custom forged pistons, balanced titanium valvetrain, individual throttle bodies, and custom dyno mapping.

PHASE 03

Coachbuilding & Cockpit Tailoring

In Modena, aluminum widebody panels are hand-wheeled and metal-finished. The interior is tailored in vegetable-tanned Bridge of Weir leather with bespoke CNC switchgear.

PHASE 04

2,000 km Black Forest Shakedown

Each completed machine undergoes 2,000 kilometers of high-elevation dynamic calibration along the B500 route before private handover at our Cologne facility.

ACTIVE COMMISSION 01 • CHASSIS KV-01/08

KV-01 "Monolith GT"

INSPECTION PERSPECTIVE:

Engineering & Coachbuilding Monograph

Carved from an authentic matching-numbers donor monocoque, the KV-01 Monolith GT represents the absolute pinnacle of air-cooled grand touring. The entire chassis is seam-welded and reinforced with integrated high-tensile titanium gussets, achieving a 65% increase in torsional rigidity over factory specifications.

The 4.0-liter naturally aspirated flat-six features custom-ground billet camshafts, titanium connecting rods, twin-plug ignition, and bespoke carbon-composite intake plenums. Power is delivered through a custom-ratio 6-speed manual transaxle equipped with a mechanical limited-slip differential.
