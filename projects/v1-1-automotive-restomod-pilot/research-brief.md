## 3. Candidate Research Landscape (12 Strategic Sources)

### Category 1: Bespoke Restomod & Coachbuilders
1. **Singer Vehicle Design** (`https://singervehicledesign.com`) — Definitive benchmark for bespoke air-cooled reimagining and obsessive leather/billet detail.
2. **Alfaholics** (`https://www.alfaholics.com`) — Master of lightweight analog performance, GTA-R carbon/billet engineering, and suspension purity.
3. **Eagle E-Types** (`https://www.eaglegb.com`) — Quintessential British coachbuilding and modern mechanical re-engineering.
4. **Touring Superleggera** (`https://www.touringsuperleggera.eu`) — Historic Italian coachbuilding, hand-beaten aluminum, bespoke patron commissions.
5. **Pagani Automobili** (`https://www.pagani.com`) — Masterclass in automotive art, titanium weave (Carbo-Titanium), and mechanical jewelry.

7. **Porsche Classic Restoration** (`https://www.porsche.com/international/accessoriesandservice/classic/`) — Archival factory restoration documentation.

### Category 3: Precision Luxury Objects (Cross-Industry)

### Category 4: Curatorial Editorial & Automotive Culture

11. **Petrolicious Classic Film Archive** (`https://petrolicious.com`) — Cinematic storytelling, natural raking light, and driving romance.

### Category 5: Curated Web Design Discovery (Landbook & Minimal Gallery)
12. **Landbook Automotive & Product Curations** (`https://land-book.com`) & **Minimal Gallery Industrial** (`https://minimal.gallery`) — Discovering cutting-edge scroll-driven interactive mechanics and luxury product storytelling.
