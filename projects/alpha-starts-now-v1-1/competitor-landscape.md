## 2. Key Synthesis Vectors
1. **The Modern Men's Editorial Formula:** High-contrast editorial typography (`Tiempos` / `Domaine` / `Fraunces` style) creates immediate intellectual credibility.
2. **Documentary Photography:** Real adult men (35–55+) in focused action establishes authenticity far better than studio fashion models or gym-bro imagery.
3. **Restrained Conversion:** Clear, uncluttered email subscription modules positioned within high-utility content rather than aggressive popups.
