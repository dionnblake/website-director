## 1. Core Engineering Capability Preserved

### Frame-Sequence / Canvas-Based Cinematic Engineering
The primary technical asset from the legacy ASN build is the **frame-sequence extraction and canvas rendering pipeline**:
- **Source Artefacts:** `assets/frames/`, `process-frames.ps1`, `animation_job.json`
- **Mechanism:** Extraction of 24fps high-resolution sequential frames from source documentary video models (e.g. Kling / Wavespeed AI), followed by compression and playback via an HTML5 `<canvas>` controlled by `requestAnimationFrame`.
- **Key Technical Advantages:**
  1. *Codec Independence:* Eliminates MP4/H.264/AV1 browser decoding inconsistencies.
  2. *Mobile Control:* Prevents mobile OS video hijacking (e.g. iOS fullscreen video controls).
  3. *Lightweight Footprint:* A 36–48 frame WebP micro-loop delivers rich ambient motion in $\approx 250\text{–}350\text{ KB}$ vs. 11.5 MB video files.
  4. *Viewport Optimization:* IntersectionObserver pauses rendering when offscreen.
