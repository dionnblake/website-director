# BRAND POSITIONING & ANTI-BRAND BOUNDARIES — ALPHA STARTS NOW

> **Schema Version:** 1.1.0  
> **Status:** Discovery Complete & Synthesized  

---

## 1. Brand Posture & Tone
* **Primary Posture (45%):** Authoritative Editorial Publication (Quiet confidence, sharp editorial typography, thoughtful pacing).
* **Secondary Posture (35%):** Pragmatic Modern Instrument (Functional clarity, structured frameworks, high utility).
* **Supporting Posture (20%):** Warm Grounded Resolve (Understated strength, timeless maturity, inviting humanity).
* **Audience Resonance:** *"This was built for a grown man who is serious about improving his life, not for someone trying to sell me an identity."*

---

## 2. Masculinity Standard
Masculinity is expressed through **restraint, competence, physical reality, typography, composition, and purposeful action** — never through tribal posturing or aggressive symbols.

---

## 3. Strict Anti-Brand Boundaries (Prohibited)
1. **Zero Manosphere / Pseudo-Masculine Tropes:** No wolves, lions, gladiators, tactical camo, skulls, flames, black/red aggression, screaming gym bros, shirtless flexing, or testosterone supplement styling.
2. **Zero Fake Wealth & Hustle Culture:** No supercars, luxury watch status flexes, private jets, crypto charts, beach-laptop hustle clichés, or artificial urgency timers.
3. **Zero Generic Affiliate Farm Appearance:** No cookie-cutter WordPress blogs, intrusive banner ad grids, endless "Top 10" cards, or SEO keyword farms.
4. **Zero AI Slop / SaaS Template Tropes:** No purple/blue SaaS gradients, floating dashboard cards, pill-button spam, giant unstyled Inter fonts, or identical 3-card loops.
5. **Zero Overdesigned Pretension:** No decorative crosshairs, coordinates, plate numbers, or monospace spam unless strictly functional.

---

## 4. Cinematic Header Mandate (Men 35+)
* **Atmosphere:** Mature documentary realism, cinematic shadow, natural lighting, brand film depth.
* **Subject Focus:** Real adult men (35–55+) in authentic purposeful action (disciplined training, grooming/dressing with care, focused work, modern tech use, daily planning, quiet reflection).
* **Motion Guardrail:** Restrained, high-performance, purposeful motion. Zero glitch effects or hyperactive transitions.
