Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Alpha Starts Now ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you subscribe to The ASN Dispatch newsletter, we collect your email address. We do not collect unnecessary personal information, tracking profiles, or demographic data.

2. How Information is Used

Your email address is used solely to deliver editorial newsletters, guides, and practical updates from Alpha Starts Now. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address to any third parties.

3. Unsubscribing & Data Deletion

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email. Upon unsubscribing, your email will be permanently removed from active mailing lists.

4. Analytics & Cookies

We may use privacy-preserving, aggregate analytics to understand overall readership patterns without tracking individual visitor behavior across the web.
