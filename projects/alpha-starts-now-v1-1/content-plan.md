## 1. Executive Editorial Strategy & Truthfulness Contract

Alpha Starts Now is an independent, content-led editorial publication and curated recommendation resource for adult men (ages 35–55+) seeking deliberate, practical personal progress.

### The Editorial Authority Model:
* **Evidence & Sourcing Foundation:** Grounded in verified research, transparent sourcing, practical analysis, and an honest founder-led editorial perspective.
* **Clear Limits on Expertise:** Alpha Starts Now explicitly does not position its author or founder as a physician, certified trainer, therapist, financial advisor, or universal expert. Content is educational and practical, not individualized professional advice.
* **Zero Fabricated Offerings:** No imaginary courses, masterminds, coaching packages, or manufactured community tiers.
* **Zero Fabricated Social Proof:** No fake testimonials, fake user counts, or artificial urgency mechanisms.
* **Commercial Transparency Principle:**  
  *"Alpha Starts Now maintains strict editorial independence. Some links may earn ASN an affiliate commission at no additional cost to the reader. Compensation does not determine whether something is recommended or how it is evaluated."*
* **Product Evaluation Policy:** Strict prohibition of purchased rankings, paid positive reviews, undisclosed sponsorships, or commission-driven manipulation. Any future review units supplied free for evaluation will be explicitly disclosed.
