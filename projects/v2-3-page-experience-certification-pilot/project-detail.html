Monograph 08 · Snaefellsnes Peninsula

Kronos Subterranean Pavilion

TECTONIC ISOMETRIC SCHEMATIC

Excavation & Geological Dialogue

Constructed over a 36-month excavation period, the Kronos Pavilion is not sited upon the terrain, but subtracted directly from it. Over 4,200 cubic meters of volcanic basalt were diamond-wire sawed to form the primary acoustic and structural envelope.

Material Provenance & Specifications

Rigorous material analysis conducted on diamond-honed basalt aggregate and custom-alloyed thermal stabilization frames.

COMPONENT MATERIAL SPECIFICATION
Primary Monolith Honed Snaefellsnes Basalt
Tension Rods Grade 5 Titanium Alloy (Ti-6Al-4V)
Acoustic Core Micro-Perforated Volcanic Ash Composite
Thermal Inertia Delta T < 0.4°C per 24h cycle
ACQUISITION & VISITS

Private architectural delegations and research access scheduled bi-annually.
