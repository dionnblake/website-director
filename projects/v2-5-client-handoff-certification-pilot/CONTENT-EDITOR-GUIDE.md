# MORROW & VALE - CONTENT EDITOR GUIDEB

> **Operational Editorial Manual for Non-Technical Maintainers**  
> **Audience:** Content Editors, Curators, and Studio Communications Team  

---

## 1. Introduction & Editorial Philosophy

Welcome to the Morrow & Vale content management system. This guide enables you to update project case studies, publish studio journal essays, and manage team profiles without technical knowledge while preserving design integrity.

---

## 2. Managing Content Types

### 2.1 Projects & Monographs
- **Title:** Keep concise and evocative (under 80 characters).
- **Summary:** Single-paragraph cognitive hook detailing location, material thesis, and building type.
- **Hero Image:** Must maintain 16:9 aspect ratio with a minimum resolution of 1920x1080px. Ensure images showcase authentic architectural lighting.
- **Alt Text:** Mandatory descriptive text for accessibility (e.g., Rammed-earth south elevation of Lumina Pavilion at dusk).

### 2.2 Studio Journal Entries
- **Headlines:** Clear, thematic titles reflecting architectural and industrial design thought leadership.
- **Author Assignment:** Select from active, published team member IDs (team-01, team-02).
- **Rich Text Standards:** Use standard paragraphs, H2/H3 subheadings, and blockquotes. Never paste formatted text containing custom inline fonts or colored text.

### 2.3 Team Profiles
- **Role & Bio:** Focus on architectural background, material specialization, and civic project leadership.
- **Portrait:** Standard 4:3 black-and-white studio portrait.

---

## 3. Workflow States: Draft, Publish, Archive

DUring editorial preparation:
- DRFAT: Work in progress. Invisible to public website visitors.
- PUBLISHED: Active on public listings and search engine indexes.
- ARCHIVED: Removed from public index listings while preserving internal references and permalinks.

---

## 4. Slug Changes & Automatic Redirects

If you change the URL slug of a PUBLISHED project (e.g., /project/lumina to /project/lumina-pavilion):
- The system automatically creates a 301 Permanent Redirect in redirects.json.
- This ensures existing bookmarks and search engine rankings do not break.

---

## 5. Accessibility & SEO Guidelines

- **Always Provide Alt Text:** Screen readers require descriptive explanations of architectural imagery.
- **Heading Hierarchy:** Do not skip levels (use H2 before H3).
- **SEO Titles:** Format as [Item Title] | Morrow & Vale. Keep meta descriptions between 120 and 155 characters.
