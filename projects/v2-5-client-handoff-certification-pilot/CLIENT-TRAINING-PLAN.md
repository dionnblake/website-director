# MORROW & VALE - CLIENT TRAINING PLAN

> **Structured Knowledge Transfer & Editorial Onboarding Plan**  

---

## 1. Module 1: Editorial Operations (Duration: 45 min)
- **Audience:** Studio Communications Team, Editors.
- **Objectives:**
  - Creating new project monographs and assigning taxonomy.
  - Adding studio journal essays.
  - Previewing draft content in the preview environment.
  - Archiving completed case studies safely.

---

## 2. Module 2: Administrative & Custodial Training (Duration: 30 min)
- **Audience:** Managing Partners, Technical Administrator.
- **Objectives:**
  - Reviewing the Digital Ownership Register.
  - Understanding backup snapshot creation and restore procedures.
  - Managing user roles and least-privilege permissions.
  - Escalation paths for design system updates.
