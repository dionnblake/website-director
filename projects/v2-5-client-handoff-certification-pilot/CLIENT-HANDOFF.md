## 1. Executive Summary & Deliverables

Morrow & Vale is equipped with a bespoke, high-performance digital presence engineered to showcase monumental cultural and civic architectural commissions. The site features an unadorned modernist aesthetic, responsive architectural typography, semantic View Transitions, and a localized structured content editing engine.

### Included Deliverables
1. **Production Codebase:** High-performance MPA architecture with zero external tracking bloat and WCAG AA accessibility compliance.
2. **Local Structured CMS:** Schema-validated content management for Projects, Team Members, and Journal Essays.
3. **Asset Vault:** Production-ready responsive imagery with full licensing provenance and zero AI-generated documentary claims.
4. **Operations Suite:** Complete documentation including ownership registers, maintenance matrices, backup scripts, release runbooks, and editor guides.

## 3. EDITORIAL BOUNDARIES: What You Can & Cannot Edit

To preserve the award-winning craft and prevent layout degradation:
- **Safely Editable:** Project titles, summaries, case study narratives, team bios, journal essays, image replacements (meeting resolution specs), and SEO titles & meta descriptions.
- **Developer Controlled:** 12-column architectural grid geometry, typographic scale ratios, CSS token definitions, View Transition choreography, and header navigation physics.
