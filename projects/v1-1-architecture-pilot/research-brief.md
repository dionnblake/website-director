## 4. Research Questions

| Category | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Industry Reality** | What does high-end architecture look like digitally, and what does an architectural patron expect on arrival? |
| **Clichés** | What visual patterns have become generic (e.g. blank single-image splash with no navigation, endless horizontal scroll, tiny illegible 8px light-grey text, flashy drone loops)? |
| **Comprehension** | Which industry conventions aid comprehension (e.g. clean project categorization, clear location/date metadata, high-resolution photography, architectural drawings/plans)? |
| **Sameness** | Which conventions create visual sameness (e.g. standard 3-column square photo grid, generic Helvetica sans headers, zero material context)? |
| **Differentiation** | Where is the visual whitespace (e.g. interactive tactile material studies, spatial daylight simulation, direct focus on stone & timber provenance, structured case studies combining blueprints and photography)? |
| **Credibility** | Which credibility cues matter most (architectural rigor, structural materiality, ETH/Politecnico pedigree, project execution depth)? |

## 6. Visual & Motion Qualities Sought
- **Visual Qualities Sought:** Warm mineral palette (Alabaster, Travertine, Umber, Bronze), generous spatial negative space, editorial type hierarchy, tactile texture details, architectural plans alongside photography.
- **Visual Qualities to Avoid:** Cold dark obsidian grids, fluorescent accents, developer marketing cards, generic Bootstrap/Tailwind layouts, stock rendering gloss.
- **Motion Qualities Sought:** Motion Level 2: Quiet, deliberate spatial transitions, subtle image zoom parallax, smooth drawer openings, respectful reduced-motion accessibility.

---

## 7. Cross-Industry Candidates
- **Adjacent Sector 1: High-End Curatorial & Architectural Publishing (Cereal Magazine, Ark Journal)** — **Why:** Asymmetric editorial layout, generous margins, tactile pagination, and literary cadence that elevates architecture to cultural art.
- **Adjacent Sector 2: Luxury Minimalist Hospitality (Aman Resorts, The Chedi Andermatt)** — **Why:** Deep atmospheric calm, warm earthy palette, and experiential storytelling.
- **Adjacent Sector 3: Precision Craft / Architectural Metal & Lighting (Viabizzuno, Boffi)** — **Why:** Relentless attention to material joints, finishes, and tactile sensory qualities.

## 8. Differentiation Questions
1. **What makes V&H instantly distinguishable?** An active synthesis of monolithic masonry architecture with an interactive tactile material laboratory and dual-layered case studies (spatial photography + architectural plans + stone provenance).
2. **What is V&H uniquely positioned to show?** The dialog between alpine stone (Valser quartzite, Engadin larch) and urban Italian precision (Milanese travertine, brushed bronze joinery).
3. **If the logo were removed, what makes this site recognizably V&H?** The warm alabaster mineral atmosphere, high-contrast serif headlines, tactile material switcher, and calm editorial rhythm.
