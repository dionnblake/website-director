THE ATELIER

Conceived in Zurich. Crafted across the Alps.

Founded in 2014 by Adrian Valentin and Elena Hesse, the atelier operates at the intersection of Swiss structural discipline and Italian spatial sensitivity.

We deliberately limit our practice to four simultaneous residential commissions annually. This boundary ensures uncompromising personal attention from initial site topography sketches to the final placement of quarried stone.

01

Permanence Over Novelty

Structures designed to endure centuries, weathering gracefully without dependence on fleeting trends.

02

Site-Specific Topography

Every floorplan is directly generated from solar angles, mountain wind corridors, and local geology.

03

Master Builder Accountability

Direct onsite craftsmanship supervision with multi-generational regional stonemasons and joiners.

AV

Adrian Valentin

Dipl. Arch. ETH SIA • Design Principal & Structural Lead

Educated at ETH Zurich under the chair of structural morphology. Former senior project architect for major cultural pavilions in Basel and Kyoto before founding V&H in 2014. Specializes in heavy stone massing, seismic structural integration, and alpine thermodynamics.

EH

Elena Hesse

M.Arch Politecnico di Milano • Interior Architecture Director

Trained in architectural restoration and bespoke spatial joinery in Milan. Leads material procurement, acoustic design, lighting choreography, and custom architectural metalwork across all Swiss and Italian residential commissions.

Alpine Primary Residence

House of Three Monoliths

Vals, Grisons, Switzerland • 2024
Perspective Mode:
Exterior South-Facing Elevation: Monolithic Valser Quartzite Massing
Structural Section Drawing AA-1: Subterranean Geothermal & Thermal Core

Spatial & Structural Philosophy

Carved directly into a steep glacial slope, House of Three Monoliths is composed of three interlocking stone volumes that shield interior living quarters from alpine winter winds while opening completely to southern valley panoramas through deep recessed bronze glazing.

Gross Floor Area: 780 m²
Primary Structural Core: Reinforced Monolithic Concrete with 180mm Cavity Insulation & Valser Quartzite Masonry
Energy & Climate System: Ground-Coupled Geothermal Probes, Controlled Heat Recovery Ventilation
Interior Joinery: Custom Alpine Larch, Ceppo di Gré Stone, Acid-Etched Bronze
