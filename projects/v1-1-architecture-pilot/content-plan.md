- **Origin:** `Vals Valley, Grisons (1,250m elevation)`

- **Tactile Profile:** `Dense, mica-flecked metamorphic slate with cool thermal inertia and subtle grey-green veining.`
- **Architectural Role:** `Foundation walls, thermal hearths, integrated wet-room basins.`

#### Specimen 02: Milanese Travertine Navona
- **Origin:** `Tivoli Quarries, Italy`
- **Finish:** `Open-pore honed matte finish`
- **Tactile Profile:** `Warm bone-white limestone with cellular sediment ribbons; reflects soft, indirect ambient daylight.`
- **Architectural Role:** `Monolithic colonnades, interior floor slabs, exterior sheltered courtyards.`

#### Specimen 03: Engadin End-Grain Alpine Larch
- **Origin:** `High-Altitude Swiss Alps`
- **Finish:** `Untreated heartwood with natural organic beeswax seal`
- **Tactile Profile:** `Tight growth rings with dense resinous grain; ages over decades into a dignified silver-grey patina.`
- **Architectural Role:** `Structural roof trusses, custom acoustic wall cladding, seamless ceiling planes.`

#### Specimen 04: Hand-Brushed Gunmetal Bronze
- **Origin:** `Artisanal Foundry, Brianza, Italy`
- **Finish:** `Chemical liver-of-sulphur patina, hand-waxed`

- **Architectural Role:** `Glazing frames, pivot door hardware, custom lighting apertures, structural tension rods.`
