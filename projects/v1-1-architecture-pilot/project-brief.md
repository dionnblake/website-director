# Project Brief: VALENTIN & HESSE Architects

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Studio Name:** VALENTIN & HESSE Architects (V&H)
- **Practice Focus:** High-end residential architecture, alpine sanctuaries, adaptive reuse of historic structures, and tailored interior architecture.
- **Locations:** Zurich (Primary Atelier) • St. Moritz / Engadin • Milan
- **Founders:** Adrian Valentin (ETH SIA) & Elena Hesse (Politecnico di Milano)
- **Design Philosophy:** "Architecture of Mass, Light & Material Silence" — spaces carved from enduring natural materials, governed by volumetric proportions, and attuned to the surrounding landscape.
- **Project Type:** Real-World Pilot #2 for Website Director V1.1 (Validating Visual Research Director, Landbook discovery, JCodesMore `RESEARCH_ONLY_MODE` deep recon, Motion Level 2 direction, Anti-Homogenization, and Independent QA Boundary).

## 2. Business Objectives & Primary Conversion
- **Primary Conversion:** Schedule a Private Project Consultation / Initial Spatial Briefing.
- **Secondary Conversion:** Request Studio Portfolio Monograph (Curated Digital PDF Portfolio).
- **Target Audience:**
  - Affluent private individuals and families commissioning custom primary residences and alpine retreats.
  - Art collectors and design-literate patrons seeking architectural homes with museum-grade light control.
  - Boutique hospitality and cultural patrons seeking singular, place-specific spaces.
- **Transaction Value Profile:** High-consideration architectural commissions (€2.5M – €25M+ construction value). Decision cycles span 3–18 months.

## 3. Scope of Services
1. **Monolithic Residential Architecture:** Ground-up custom residences, lakeside villas, and alpine retreats.
2. **Interior Architecture & Spatial Design:** Bespoke joinery, custom stone masonry, integrated lighting, and acoustic planning.
3. **Adaptive Reuse & Restoration:** Sensitive transformation of historical farmhouses, industrial lofts, and heritage estates.
4. **Material Specification & Procurement:** Direct sourcing of regional stones, timber, and hand-finished architectural metals.
