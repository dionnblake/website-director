The Daily Rituals for Adult Men

You do not need a three-hour morning routine. You need three simple, non-negotiable anchors that survive a demanding workweek.

RITUAL 01 // 06:30

The Hydration & Light Anchor

500ml water with sea salt and 10 minutes of direct morning sunlight before checking any digital screen.

RITUAL 02 // 12:00

The Midday Mobility Reset

5 minutes of thoracic extension and hip flexor stretches to decompress spinal compression from desk work.

RITUAL 03 // 21:30

The Analog Evening Shutdown

3-line handwritten review of today's executed priorities and deliberate preparation for tomorrow.
