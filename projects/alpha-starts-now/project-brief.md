## 1. Business Core
- **Company / Brand Name:** Alpha Starts Now (ASN)
- **One-Sentence Proposition:** A trusted, mature guide and filter for adult men (35+) who are actively improving their physical standards, discipline, mindset, habits, and life direction without hype, fake masculinity, or guru culture.
- **Business Model & Core Offerings (V1):**
  - **Content-Led Authority & Editorial Guides:** Practical, high-utility content across health, physical appearance, habits, discipline, environment, career/financial direction, and time management.
  - **Curated Recommendations & Affiliate Integration:** Editorially selected tools, gear, products, and services that solve real problems for adult men.
  - **Owned Audience (Email Publication):** Ongoing high-value newsletter delivering consistent, actionable guidance directly to subscribers.
  - *Explicit Constraint:* Zero fabricated products, memberships, courses, masterminds, or fake coaching offers.
- **Target Customer / ICP:**
  - **Primary Decision Maker:** Adult men approximately 35+ who know they need to improve, are past the quick-fix/gym-bro phase, and want serious, grounded, practical progress.
  - **Psychographic Profile:** Thoughtful, ambitious, self-aware, seeking discipline and deliberate control over their trajectory. Tired of hyper-aggressive "manosphere" rage, 22-year-old fitness influencers, and fake alpha tropes.
- **Primary Conversion Objective:** Join the Alpha Starts Now email newsletter (build trusted owned audience).
- **Secondary Conversion Objectives:**
  - Explore foundational reading ("Start Here").
  - Engage with curated tools and resources ("Recommended").
  - Read the core philosophy ("About / Manifesto").
  - Access relevant external affiliate recommendations naturally.

- **The Core Philosophy / Superpower:**

## 3. Scope & Page Footprint (V1)
- **Website Scope:** Focused Multi-Page Editorial & Commercial Brand Website.
- **Core Route Architecture:**
  1. **Home (`/`):** Primary brand introduction, core thesis, key content pillars, featured curated tools, and dominant email subscription engine.
  2. **Start Here (`/start-here`):** Orientation path helping new visitors diagnose their friction points and begin their deliberate improvement journey.
  3. **Guides & Content (`/guides` or `/content`):** Structured practical guides organized by meaningful life domains (Health, Habits, Appearance, Career/Finance, Lifestyle).
  4. **Recommended (`/recommended`):** Editorially curated tools, gear, and resources with transparent utility and integrity.
  5. **About & Manifesto (`/about`):** Why ASN exists, what it believes, who it is for, and what it refuses to become.
  6. **Legal & Governance (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/disclaimer`):** Transparent privacy policy, terms of service, and clear FTC-compliant affiliate disclosures.
