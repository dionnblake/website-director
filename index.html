THE TURNING POINT Recognition before instruction

You have carried a lot.

You kept showing up for everyone else.

Work. Family. Bills. The thousand small things that keep a life moving.

Somewhere in the middle of handling it all, your own baseline changed. Your energy got thinner. Your confidence got quieter. The old routines stopped fitting the man you are now.

That is not failure. It is information.
